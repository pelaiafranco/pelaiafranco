**Descripción:**
```
Split profits between investors and sponsor the way institutional funds do: pref, IRR hurdle and promote, year by year.

WHAT IT DOES
✔ Tier 1: return of capital + compounding preferred return, paid pro rata
✔ Tier 2: GP promote up to an LP IRR hurdle (e.g., 80/20 up to 13%)
✔ Tier 3: GP promote above the hurdle (e.g., 70/30)
✔ GP co-invest handled correctly: co-invest earns its pro-rata share in every tier
✔ LP and GP IRR, equity multiple, profit share and promote earned
✔ Every dollar allocated, with a built-in check that must equal $0
✔ Exit-price scenario lever and stacked chart of distributions by year
```
